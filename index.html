WHAT IS XP ENTERTAINMENT AGENCY?

Founded in 2024, XP Entertainment Agency is a creative marketing and entertainment partnerships company built to elevate brands, businesses, and talent.

From nightclubs, lounges, and lifestyle businesses to athletes, artists, and influencers, XP brings together creative design, digital marketing, event promotion, talent booking, and strategic partnerships.

Our mission is simple: turn visibility into opportunity, ideas into experiences, and connections into growth.
